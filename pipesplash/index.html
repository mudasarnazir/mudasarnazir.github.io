Pipe Splash Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This policy explains how Pipe Splash handles information when you play the mobile game. Pipe Splash is provided by Mudasar Nazir.

Information stored on your device

Pipe Splash saves puzzle progress, settings, ad preferences, and your acceptance of the in-app terms and privacy notice on your device. The game does not require a Pipe Splash account. On iOS, Apple Game Center provides platform sign-in and iCloud Key-Value Storage syncs the puzzle progress snapshot. On Android, Google Play Games provides sign-in and its Saved Games service syncs the snapshot. Synced progress can be restored on another device using the same platform account. Pipe Splash does not operate its own game account or progress server. Resetting progress creates a reset for the same platform save; if the device is offline or signed out, that change syncs when the platform service becomes available again.

Advertising

Pipe Splash may show banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads using AppLovin MAX. AppLovin and its advertising partners may process information such as your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, ad requests and interactions, and diagnostic information to serve ads, measure performance, and help prevent fraud. Their handling of that information is described in the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

On Apple devices, Pipe Splash may ask for permission through App Tracking Transparency. Your choice affects the advertising signals available to the ad service. You can review the app's ad privacy choices in Pipe Splash settings and change tracking permission in your device settings. On Android, you can manage advertising identifiers and personalization through your device settings.

Sharing and retention

When platform sign-in and sync are available, Apple or Google processes platform identity and saved progress under its own account and service terms. Advertising information is handled by AppLovin and its partners under their policies and retention practices. Pipe Splash does not operate its own account or analytics service.

Children

Pipe Splash does not ask players to provide personal information directly. If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern about a child using the game, contact us and we will review the request.

Your choices and contact

Uninstalling stops Pipe Splash from making new ad requests but does not delete information an advertising provider has already collected. Use the provider's policy and your device settings for access, deletion, or advertising choices. For privacy questions or requests, contact Mudasar Nazir at mudasar80@gmail.com.
