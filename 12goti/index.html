Privacy Policy — 12 Goti

Effective Date: October 25, 2026  |  App: 12 Goti (com.nexiumlabs.twelvegoti)  |  Developer / Controller: Mudasar Nazir

This Privacy Policy explains how 12 Goti ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you play on iOS or Android.

1. Summary

  • No Accounts: 12 Goti does not require or offer user registration, login accounts, profiles, or cloud saves.
  • Offline Play Collects Nothing: Single-device play (vs Computer and 2-player pass-and-play) runs entirely on your device and sends no gameplay data over the network.
  • Optional Online Rooms: If you open Online mode to play with a friend using an 8-character room code, moves are relayed transiently over a Supabase Realtime Broadcast channel. No accounts are created and no moves or games are stored in any database; standard WebSocket connection metadata (such as your IP address) is visible to the relay server during the session.
  • Ad-Supported: In builds with advertising enabled, we display ads via AppLovin MAX and its mediation partners in single-device modes. No banner or other ads appear in online rooms, except (a) an opt-in rewarded video (with an interstitial fallback) that a stalled online game (50 turns without a capture) asks both players to watch to keep the room going—and if no video can be shown within 45 seconds, the game ends as a draw—and (b) an interstitial ad that may appear when leaving a finished online game.
  • Age Rating (13+): 12 Goti is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The App does not participate in Apple Kids or Google Play Families programs.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores local preferences and game state solely on your device using platform local storage (SharedPreferences on Android / NSUserDefaults on iOS):

  • Game settings (opponent mode, computer difficulty, first move, compulsory capture).
  • Your current local board position and move history so you can resume an unfinished game.
  • Hint wallet balance (3–5 hint credits) and legal/privacy consent state.

This local data never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear its storage.

3. Optional Online Play (Room Codes)

When you choose the Online opponent option and create or join a room code:

  • Your device connects over TLS WebSockets to a Supabase Realtime Broadcast channel derived from the room code.
  • Only signed game protocol messages (handshake, turn number, board move coordinates, rematch/draw/resign signals, and stall-checkpoint status signals) are broadcast between the two devices in the room.
  • No user names, email addresses, device identifiers, or chat messages are transmitted, and no moves or match histories are written to any database table.
  • As with any internet connection, the relay infrastructure (Supabase / Cloudflare) processes standard network connection data such as your IP address and connection timestamps to route the WebSocket traffic and enforce rate limits.

4. Advertising & AppLovin MAX

To keep 12 Goti free, single-device modes display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads (for hints) through the AppLovin MAX SDK and its mediated ad networks. Ads are never initialized or requested until after you accept the in-app Terms & Privacy notice. In Online mode, no banner or hint ads appear in online rooms, except (a) an opt-in rewarded video (with an interstitial fallback) that a stalled online game (50 consecutive turns without a capture) asks both players to watch to keep the room going—and if no video can be shown within 45 seconds, the game ends as a neutral draw—and (b) an interstitial ad that may appear when leaving a finished online game (for example on Rematch, Leave room, or New game).

Depending on your platform and privacy choices, AppLovin MAX and its partners may collect or process:

  • Device and network information: IP address (used for coarse geolocation and fraud prevention), device make/model, operating system version, screen size, language, and mobile carrier.
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data: ad impressions, clicks, reward completions, crash logs, and SDK performance metrics.
  • Advertising identifiers (subject to permission): IDFA on iOS (only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission) or Google Advertising ID on Android.

5. iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) & Android Limited Ads

  • iOS: On iOS 14.5+, right after you accept the in-app Terms & Privacy notice, the App shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. If you allow tracking, AppLovin MAX is configured with user consent (setHasUserConsent(true)) and may use the IDFA to serve personalized ads. If you decline, or if tracking is restricted or unavailable, the App configures setHasUserConsent(false) and requests limited (non-personalized) ads without accessing the IDFA. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Ads, or in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: The App configures AppLovin MAX in Limited Ads mode by default (setHasUserConsent(false) and setDoNotSell(true)), instructing the SDK and mediated networks to serve non-personalized / opt-out ads.

For details on how AppLovin processes data, please review the AppLovin Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy (13+)

12 Goti is intended for users aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us at the email below.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA / CPRA)

Because we do not maintain user accounts or store personal profiles on our own servers, we do not hold a database of your personal information. You can exercise your privacy choices at any time by:

  • Switching between Personalized and Limited ads in Settings → Privacy & Ads (on iOS) or revoking ATT permission in iOS Settings.
  • Resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device's system privacy settings.
  • Uninstalling the App to erase all locally stored game and settings data.
