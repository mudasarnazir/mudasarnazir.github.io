Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I play Online with a friend?
    Open Settings → Opponent → Online. One player taps Create room and shares the 8-character room code. The second player enters that code and taps Join room.
  • Why can't I move a goti when a capture is open?
    When Compulsory capture is turned on in Settings, any open jump must be taken and a jumping goti must finish its capture chain. You can toggle Compulsory capture off in Settings for local games or before starting an online room as host.
  • How do Hint credits work?
    You start with 3 hint credits and earn +1 credit (up to 5) each time you defeat the Computer. If you have 0 credits left, you can watch a short ad (or receive a free hint when no ad is available) to see a suggested move.
  • How do I manage my ad privacy preferences?
    Open Settings → Privacy & Ads to view your current ad mode, read the Privacy Policy and Terms, or change your personalized/limited ad setting on iOS.
